DuckCount is an app that helps you manage shared expenses privately. Whether you're on vacation with friends or at a restaurant with a colleague, you can be sure that everyone will pay their fair share.

Share expenses your way
• Groups of up to 100 people, for a trip, a flatshare or regular dinners
• One-off expenses, no group needed
• Split evenly, or set exactly what each person owes
• Choose the currency of every expense
• See at a glance who owes whom, and settle a group in the fewest payments
• Mark expenses as settled once they are paid back
• Every edit is kept in the history, and deleted group expenses can be restored

Private by design
• Expenses, groups and profiles are end-to-end encrypted: only the people involved can read them, not even us
• Groups are encrypted with MLS, with post-quantum protection
• Usernames reach our servers only as a fingerprint: only someone who already knows yours can find you
• Your password is checked against known leaks without ever being sent

Good to know
• Push notifications come through UnifiedPush, using any compatible distributor. On a phone with none, they go through Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging if Google Play Services is installed. Without either, the app still syncs each time you open it.
• Accounts can only be created on a recent phone with a locked bootloader, running its official system or GrapheneOS.
